# Plan: prismarine_replay

From [the replay design](../specs/2026-11-23-replay-design.md). The work is on branch `prismreplay` in both
repositories. The coordinator lands prismarine first, then mcsm with the pin. Each step keeps mcsm's routes, snapshots
and fingerprints byte-identical.

## Step 1: the replay core in prismarine

1. `packages/prismarine_replay` in the workspace, pure Dart, on `prismarine_snap`.
2. `route_hash.dart`: the FNV-1a line hash, its seed and its 16-digit hex text.
3. `route.dart`: `RouteFormat`, `HostChange`, `RouteRecorder` and `RouteReader`.
4. `sim.dart`: `ReplaySim` and `ReplayGame`.
5. `snapshot_format.dart`: `SnapshotFormat` with open, list, latest, header, file bytes, synchronous and isolate writes
   and pruning. `SnapshotFile`, the file name and the size text.
6. `snapshot_schedule.dart` and `snapshot_tail.dart`.
7. `replay_start.dart`: `openReplay`, `ReplayStart` and `ReplaySnapshots`.
8. `shadow.dart`: `runShadow`, `shadowMain` and `ShadowProcess`.
9. Tests for the hash of known lines, the line text of the recorder, resume, the reader's hashes and skips, the header
   text, the file bytes, pruning, the schedule, the tail and the shadow loop with a small sim. A README with an example
   and an area page.

## Step 2: mcsm's persist code on the core

1. `recording.dart`: `HostChange` implements the interface, `InputRecorder` extends `RouteRecorder`, `InputReplay`
   extends `RouteReader`. The header and frame maps keep their keys and order.
2. `snapshot_file.dart`: `gameSnapshots`, the format, and `GameSnapshot` over `SnapshotFile` with the log counts.
   `snapshotHeader` passes the log counts and the scene as game fields. The folder rule of runs, dumps and saves stays.
3. `snapshot_replay.dart` becomes `GameReplay` and `GameReplaySim`. `snapshot_shadow.dart` keeps the player registry
   and runs the core's shadow loop.
4. `apps/mcsm_shadow` calls `shadowMain`. The player's `snapshot_shadow.dart` keeps only the lookup of the executable
   and `sim_host.dart` starts a `ShadowProcess`. The player's schedule reads a `GameReplaySim`.
5. The corpus worker, `tool/corpus/replay.dart`, writes its snapshots through `ReplaySnapshots`.
6. The format pages `docs/formats/port/route.md` and `snapshot.md` and the area pages link to prismarine's page.

## Step 3: the corpus runner

1. `package:prismarine_replay/corpus.dart`: the line reader and gzip writer, chunks and solo costs, the divergence
   record and its report lines, the expected note, the run list, the pool, the compile step and the commands.
2. mcsm's `tool/corpus.dart` keeps its usage text and commands and runs the core's runner with `McsmCorpus`, its
   `CorpusGame`. The worker, the fingerprint, the comparison and `diff` stay in mcsm.
3. `tool/corpus_remote.sh` in the package. mcsm's `corpus_ws.sh` keeps its commands and sets the workstation, the
   container and the slice copy, then calls it from the pinned checkout.

## Checks

- The tests, analyze, format, code_shape and the alloc lint of both repositories. The alloc total across both does not
  grow.
- On the workstation, with one short corpus run: `corpus_ws.sh check <tree> --only=<id>` on the branch, and the
  route and snapshot bytes of a headless replay by `main` and by the branch.
- The coordinator runs D1, the land gate and the whole corpus before landing.
